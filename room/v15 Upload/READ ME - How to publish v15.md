# Publishing the Room Designer v15

**What's new in v15:**
- Tables removed in all four rooms, so carpet covers the whole floor.
- Real beer and team logos are gone.
- The real Monte Cristo logo sits on the blank wall panel.
- New "In the Gaming Area" view.
- The page now says "Design Your Gaming Area" and "sample design".

I tested it on the live page, using your browser only, with all four rooms, both views, carpet, marble, "gaming room only" coverage and wallpaper. Nothing on the site has changed yet.

## Step 1: GitHub (about 2 minutes)

1. Open github.com/montecristogaming/mcg-igb-data and click into the **room** folder.
2. Click **Add file › Upload files**.
3. Drag the whole **v15** folder (inside "1 - GitHub - drag into the room folder") onto the page. It has 41 files, about 3.7 MB.
4. Click **Commit changes**.

## Step 2: HubSpot Files (about 1 minute)

1. In HubSpot, go to **Content › Files** and find **room14-designer.txt**.
2. Open its menu, choose **Replace**, and pick the **room14-designer.txt** in "2 - HubSpot Files". It has the same name on purpose: replacing it keeps the address your Squarespace page already loads. No Squarespace edit is needed.

## Step 3: Check

Open montecristogaming.com/design-your-casino-room in a private window. You should see:
- "Design Your Gaming Area"
- the Room View / In the Gaming Area toggle
- the new rooms

HubSpot can take a few minutes to show the replaced file. Tell Claude when it's done and Claude will check the live page.

## Undo

A backup of the old v14 file is in the Room Designer folder: "room14-designer-live (backup).txt". Replace the HubSpot file with it to go back.
